Our Manufacturing Locations

To maximize efficiency and isolate high-precision machining from heavy fabrication, we run two units in Bangalore's industrial hubs.

UNIT 01

Prakashnagar Facility

Headquarters & Pressed Sheet Metal Shop
  • Address: No. 63/121, 9th Main Road, Prakashnagar, Rajajinagar, Behind Petrol Bunk, Bangalore - 560021
  • Area: ~8,000 Sq. Ft. production floor
  • Core Operations: Shearing, heavy mechanical pressing, compound die stamping, MIG/TIG/Spot welding, surface preparation, wet painting, and powder coating curing.
  • Focus: Primary structural sheet metal parts and sub-assembly stamping.
UNIT 02

Lakshminarayanapuram Facility

Precision Machining & Quality Metrology Cell
  • Address: No. M-14, 8th Cross, Lakshminarayanapuram, Bangalore - 560021
  • Area: ~4,000 Sq. Ft. specialized workspace
  • Core Operations: 3-Axis Vertical Machining (VMC), high-precision CNC turning and lathe profiling, custom thread tapping, component inspection, and metrology calibration.
  • Focus: Complex components with close tolerance parameters.

Facility Organization

Lean Workplace Organization

We execute manufacturing orders following lean B2B workplace protocols to guarantee structural safety, material traceability, and minimal process waste.

5S Clean Manufacturing Zones

Our CNC machining and press lines follow structured workplace organization to prevent material contamination and reduce tool-hunting lead times.

Material Safety & Segregation

Alloys, sheet steels, and machined parts are color-coded and stored in designated zones with material test certificates (MTC) tracked continuously.

Operator & Facility Safety

Our staff operates using safety gear (PPE). We maintain clean exhaust ventilation systems across our welding and paint booths.
