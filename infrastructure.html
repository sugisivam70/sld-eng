Metrology Lab

Quality & Inspection Instruments

Our temperature-controlled quality control room features high-precision metrology equipment and calibrated measuring instruments to verify aerospace & defense tolerances down to the micron level.

Metrology Master (01 No.)

Electronica 2D Ultima M 700

High-precision 2D motorized Height Master featuring 700mm measuring range, automated 2D feature processing (bore centers, pitch, flatness, perpendicularity), and sub-micron resolution.

  • 700mm vertical measuring range
  • Motorized touch-probe with 2D feature math
  • Sub-micron height & distance accuracy
Dimensional Inspection

Digital Micrometers

Precision outside micrometers covering 0–25mm and 25–50mm range with carbide-tipped measuring faces for high-repeatability wall thickness and shaft diameter checks.

  • 0–25mm & 25–50mm measuring range
  • 0.001mm digital display resolution
  • Carbide-tipped anvil & spindle faces
Precision Gauging

Vernier & Digital Calipers

Mitutoyo precision digital and analog calipers covering 0–200mm and 0–300mm for rapid internal, external, depth, and step measurements on shopfloor and QA lab.

  • 0–200mm & 0–300mm measuring scale
  • Absolute encoder technology
  • Stainless steel hardened construction
Bore & Runout Metrology

Bore Gauges & Dial Indicators

High-sensitivity dial test indicators (0.001mm) and internal dial bore gauges for precise concentricity, run-out, circularity, and cylinder bore diameter inspection.

  • Internal bore diameter measurement
  • High-sensitivity 0.001mm dial indicators
  • Magnetic bases & alignment stands
Thread Verification

26 Calibrated Thread Gauges

Comprehensive inventory of 26 calibrated Go/No-Go thread plug and thread ring gauges (SLD-TPG-01 to SLD-TPG-26) covering Metric, UN, and NPT thread profiles.

  • 26 Calibrated Gauge Sets (SLD-TPG-01 ~ 26)
  • Go / No-Go pitch diameter verification
  • Traceable to NABL calibration standards

Slip Gauges & Pin Gauge Sets

Facility Organization

Lean Workplace Organization

We execute manufacturing orders following lean B2B workplace protocols to guarantee structural safety, material traceability, and minimal process waste.
