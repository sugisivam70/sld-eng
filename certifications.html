National & International Credentials

Our certifications signify operational transparency, environmental responsibility, and compliance with domestic and international trade protocols.

QMS STANDARD

ISO 9001:2015 Certification

Registrar: Accredited QMS Auditor
Registration No: SRI/QMS/9001-2025
Scope: Precision sheet metal fabrication, machining, surface coating, and component assemblies.

Highlights our dedication to consistent quality improvement and customer satisfaction. All fabrication stages — from raw steel nesting and shearing to final welding, powder coating, and dimensional metrology — adhere to structured process guides and documentation.

MSME STANDARD

ZED Bronze Certification

Registrar: Ministry of MSME, Govt of India
Certificate No: ZED-MSME-2024-BRZ
Scope: Zero Defect Manufacturing processes with Zero Effect on the surrounding environment.

Reflects our commitment to quality production processes and sustainable manufacturing. Under ZED guidelines, we employ optimal nesting plans to minimize scrap sheet metal, maintain low noise pollution in our press shop, and practice responsible chemical waste containment.

STATE TRADE PROMOTION

VTPC Registration

Registrar: Visvesvaraya Trade Promotion Centre
Registration No: VTPC-KAR-EXP-088
Scope: Export trade promotion support and state-level OEM supply chain integration in Karnataka.

Establishes our alignment with state government export networks. VTPC registration qualifies us for industrial incentives, trade expo representation, and export facilitation, bolstering our capabilities as a reliable domestic partner for international engineering firms.

GLOBAL TRADE LICENSE

Import & Export Certificate (IEC)

Registrar: Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT)
IEC Number: IEC-0700034291
Scope: Direct import of engineering raw alloy grades and global export of precision metal parts.

Authorizes direct international trade. With our active IEC license, we purchase high-grade sheet metal directly from international mills and export finished sub-assemblies to global clients in the automotive, energy, and electronics sectors.

Metrology & Verification

Zero-Defect Quality Metrology

At Sri Radhai Industries, certifications are backed by rigorous daily inspection protocols. We operate a temperature-controlled metrology area to ensure exact dimensional conformance.

Vernier Calipers & Micrometers

Regularly calibrated measuring instruments to verify pressed thickness and turned diameters down to ±0.02mm tolerance.

Precision Height Gauges

Verifying planar flatness, bend offsets, and overall component heights on critical automotive structural brackets.

Thread & Plug Gauges

Verifying interior and exterior thread dimensions for turned studs, fasteners, and custom bushings.

Calibration Logs

All inspection tools are tracked on calibration sheets to maintain absolute traceability for compliance audits.
