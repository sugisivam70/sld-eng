Quality Framework

Our Quality Assurance Pillars

Four fundamental principles that guide consistent production and conformity to customer-approved specifications.

Dimensional Accuracy

We use calibrated measuring instruments, gauges, and CMM inspection systems to verify critical dimensions and tight tolerances against approved technical drawings.

Process Control

From raw-material verification and in-process checks to final inspection, defined procedures help identify deviations early and minimize variations.

Documentation & Traceability

We maintain thorough inspection records and production documentation for every project, supporting complete component traceability and quality verification.

Standards Compliance

We follow strict quality standards and agreed customer requirements throughout manufacturing, checking components against specified acceptance criteria prior to delivery.

Quality Control Lab

In-House Quality Control Lab

At SLD Engineering, our in-house quality control lab uses calibrated inspection instruments to ensure dimensional accuracy, surface finish, and mating feature integrity across aerospace, defence, medical, oil & gas, and electronics components.

Vernier Calipers & Micrometers

Measuring component dimensions, wall thicknesses, and turned outer/inner diameters.

Precision Height Gauges

Checking component heights, step offsets, and feature positions relative to surface plates.

Bore & Thread Gauges

Checking internal bore diameters and verifying conformity to thread specifications.

Surface Roughness Testers & CMM

Measuring surface textures and performing CMM inspection for complex component geometries.
