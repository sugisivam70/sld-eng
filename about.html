Our History

A Legacy Built on Quality

Operating from our facility in Bangalore, India, Sri Radhai Industries has been a trusted supplier in the precision engineering sector for over 25 years.

Our journey began with a simple commitment: delivering high-performance components manufactured strictly to client specifications. Over the years, we have scaled our processes and machinery to meet growing industrial demands while maintaining an uncompromising focus on quality, cost-efficiency, and on-time fulfillment.

Under the direct supervision and discipline of our proprietor, we ensure a zero-defect production standard. This hands-on management, coupled with a highly skilled workforce of technicians and engineers, has established us as a competitive B2B manufacturing partner.

Our Journey

Milestones of Progress

A chronology of precision, growth, and industrial adaptation over the past two decades.

2000

Incorporation & Foundation

Sri Radhai Industries was founded in Bangalore with a vision to deliver high-quality pressed sheet metal components. Started with a single workshop and manual press machine, focusing on local manufacturing clients.

2012

Infrastructure Expansion

Significant scale-up of manufacturing space and acquisition of specialized pneumatic and hydraulic presses. Introduced welding divisions and began supplying to regional OEMs across South India.

2025

Advanced CNC Integration

Deployed high-precision CNC lathe and turning centers. Achieved full B2B compliance standards and transitioned to offering end-to-end engineered assemblies with proprietor-led zero-defect tolerances.
