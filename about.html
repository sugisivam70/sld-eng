Company Profile

Trusted Precision Manufacturing Partner

SLD Engineering, located in Peenya, Bengaluru, is dedicated to delivering high-precision components and turnkey manufacturing solutions across high-technology sectors.

We specialize in manufacturing precision components for aerospace, defence, engineering, oil and gas, medical implants, electronic components, and rapid prototypes. Our skilled workforce works with exact precision to satisfy customer-approved drawings, technical specifications, and strict quality standards.

Equipped with advanced CNC machinery, high-speed vertical machining centers (STM VL610), CNC turning lathes, gear cutting tools, and modern in-house testing equipment, we maintain stringent quality control at every stage of production. Our commitment to accuracy, reliable service, and on-time delivery makes SLD Engineering a dependable partner for prototype development through volume production.
