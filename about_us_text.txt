ABOUT US    Precision Manufacturing. Consistent Quality.
G.T. Engineering Solutions , established in    2021 , is an    ISO-certified company    located in Peenya, Bengaluru. The company is led by its Managing Proprietor,    Mr. Girish G.T. , who brings    15 years of experience in CNC component manufacturing .
We specialize in manufacturing    aerospace components, defence components, engineering components, oil and gas industry components, medical implants, electronic components and rapid prototypes . Our team of    15 skilled employees    works with precision and dedication to meet demanding technical specifications and customer requirements.
Equipped with    advanced CNC machinery and modern testing equipment , we maintain stringent quality standards at every stage of production. Our commitment to accuracy, reliable service and    on-time delivery    enables us to provide high-quality manufacturing solutions to customers across a wide range of industries.
