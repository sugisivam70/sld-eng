Pressed Sheet Metal

High-precision pressing, bending, and forming utilizing hydraulic and pneumatic press lines tailored for custom OEM specifications.

  • Hydraulic Bending (Up to 100 Tons)
  • Progressive & Compound Dies
  • Steel, Brass & Aluminum sheet processing
02

CNC Turning & Lathe Work

Computer-controlled lathe machining targeting tight dimensional tolerances and high-quality surface finishes on turned parts.

  • High accuracy spindle speeds
  • Tolerance limits within ±0.02mm
  • Bar feeding capabilities for mass runs
03

MIG, TIG & Spot Welding

Robust component assembly and welding services ensuring high shear strength joints ready for structural load conditions.

  • Specialized MIG/TIG setups
  • Pneumatic spot-welding units
  • Welded fixture alignment checks
04

Liquid Surface Painting

Wet-spray coating for surface protection, providing clean, uniform finishes that resist environmental oxidation.

  • Anti-corrosion primer coatings
  • Dust-controlled spray booths
  • Custom synthetic & polyurethane enamel
05

Powder Coating (Oven Cured)

Dry powder surface finishing cured in heating ovens, creating a thick, durable outer skin resistant to chipping and chemical wear.

  • Electrostatic powder application
  • High temp thermal curing chambers
  • Uniform micron thickness auditing

End-to-end mechatronic and mechanical sub-assemblies. We deliver finished assemblies directly to OEM assembly lines.

  • Hardware & component integration

  • JIT (Just-In-Time) shipping lines

OEM Workflow

The Manufacturing Process

A step-by-step roadmap from drawing analysis to finished product delivery.

01

Drawing & Material Audit

Detailed evaluation of client blueprints, geometric tolerances, material grades, and specifications. Sourcing approved raw materials with test certifications.

02

Tooling & Programming

Creating tool designs and programming CNC/VMC machineries. Custom tooling dies are fabricated to match specialized bending geometries.

03

Prototyping & Inspection

Fabricating the first-article sample. The sample undergoes metrology dimension inspection. Production lines are only initiated after client approval.

04

Precision Production

Initiating automated sheet metal press lines, welding cells, or CNC setups. Quality audits are conducted at intermediate stage intervals.

05

Finishing & Dispatch

Applying surface treatments (painting/powder coating) followed by final metrology validation. Parts are packaged and dispatched directly to your assembly floor.
