Manufacturing Workflow

Stage-Wise Production Process

A structured roadmap from drawing audit to final CMM verification and on-time delivery.

01

Drawing & Requirement Review

We review customer drawings, material specifications, quantities, and delivery expectations to define technical scope and prevent misunderstandings.

02

Production Planning & Tooling

Planning CNC machining operations, tool design, and inspection stages around material availability and machine capacity.

03

Precision CNC Machining

Components are manufactured using advanced CNC milling (STM VL610), turning lathe centers, and controlled processes with in-process dimension checks.

04

Special Processes & Finishing

Surface treatments including anodizing, plating, heat treatment, powder coating, and black oxide according to customer requirements.

05

Metrology Lab Inspection

Final inspection in our temperature-controlled quality lab using CMM, height gauges, micrometers, and roughness testers.

06

Turnkey Assembly & Delivery

Carefully assembled sub-components, packaged with inspection documentation, and delivered on-time to client facilities.
